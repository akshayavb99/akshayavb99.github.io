I like solving problems, experimenting with ideas, and understanding what's happening under the hood.

My background is in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (UC Berkeley), and my interests have gradually taken me from data analytics into the world of AI and machine learning.

These days, you'll find me exploring LLMs, building AI-powered applications, reading research papers, and occasionally spending far too long debugging something.

This website is a collection of that journey — the projects, the experiments, the [notes](https://akshayavb99.github.io/learning-notes/), and everything in between.
